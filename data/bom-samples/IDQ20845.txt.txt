Final Flood Warning for the Warrego River (QLD)

Issued at 11:36 am AEST on Friday 13 November 2026
Flood Warning Number: 25


MINOR FLOODING NO LONGER OCCURRING AT ROCKY

The river level along the lower Warrego River at Rocky is estimated to have fallen below
the minor flood level and is expected to remain below the minor flood level.
A minor flood warning is current for the Warrego River (NSW).

Warrego River downstream of Charleville:
Flooding has eased along the lower Warrego River at Rocky.

No recent river level observations are available for the Warrego River at Rocky. Based on
upstream stations, the river level is estimated to have fallen below the minor flood level
(2.50 m). The Warrego River at Rocky is expected to remain below the minor flood level.

Safety Advice:
* Don't drive, walk, swim or play in floodwater because it is dangerous.
* Stay away from flooded drains, rivers, streams and waterways.
* Obey road closure signs. Plan ahead so you don't drive on flooded roads.
* Check the ABC and local media for updates. The situation can change quickly, so stay
informed.
* For local emergency management warnings and advice visit
www.disaster.qld.gov.au/warnings.

For emergency assistance call SES on telephone number 132 500. In life-threatening
emergencies, call 000 (triple zero) immediately.

Next issue:
This is a final warning. No further warnings will be issued for this event.
