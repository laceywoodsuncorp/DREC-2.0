 About this table
 The rainfall observations are real-time operational data, and received from a mixture of manual readings and automated telemetry systems.
 Stations displaying empty cells have no recent observations recorded.
 Stations with multiple sensors will display data from the preferred sensor. If observations are unavailable for the preferred sensor, which is usually the highest quality and most reliable data, observations from the secondary sensors will automatically be used, where available.
 The data is provided for flood warning purposes, and it is possible that some data will not be available during non-flood periods.
 Some rainfall data is provided to the Bureau of Meteorology by other agencies. Use of these data is subject to the terms and conditions of the supplying organisations.
