Minor Flood Warning for the Warrego River (NSW)

at Barringun and Enngonia

Issued at 12:44 pm Australian Eastern Daylight Time on Friday 13 November 2026
Flood Warning Number: 11


MINOR FLOODING OCCURRING AT BARRINGUN AND ENNGONIA

A minor flood peak along Warrego River is approaching Enngonia, where river levels are
likely to remain above the minor flood level during the weekend. Further downstream, river
levels are rising at Fords Bridge. Predictions for Fords Bridge will be provided once the
flood peak is observed at Enngonia.

Warrego River (NSW):
Minor flooding is occurring along the Warrego River at Barringun and Enngonia.

The Warrego River at Barringun No. 2 is currently at 2.85 m and falling, with minor
flooding. The Warrego River at Barringun No. 2 may fall below the minor flood level (2.80
m) Friday evening.

The Warrego River at Enngonia was at 2.22 m and rising slowly at 8:00 am Friday, with
minor flooding. The Warrego River at Enngonia may peak near 2.30 m Friday evening, with
minor flooding. The river may remain above the minor flood level (2.00 m) until Monday.

Safety Advice:
* Don't drive, walk, swim or play in floodwater because it is dangerous.
* Stay away from flooded drains, rivers, streams and waterways.
* Obey road closure signs. Plan ahead so you don't drive on flooded roads.
* Check the ABC and local media for updates. The situation can change quickly, so stay
informed.
* For local emergency management warnings and advice visit www.ses.nsw.gov.au.

For emergency assistance call SES on telephone number 132 500. In life-threatening
emergencies, call 000 (triple zero) immediately.

Next issue:
The next warning will be issued by 01:00 PM AEDT on Monday 16 November 2026.
