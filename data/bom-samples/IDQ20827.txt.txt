Minor Flood Warning for the Upper Condamine River

Issued at 11:43 am AEST on Saturday 14 November 2026
Flood Warning Number: 2


MINOR FLOODING OCCURRING AT TUMMAVILLE

Thunderstorms have produced heavy rainfall since Friday afternoon in parts of the Upper
Condamine River catchment.  River and creek levels are rising and isolated flooding is
occurring. Minor flooding is occurring at Tummaville.

Further rainfall is forecast over the Upper Condamine catchment in the next few days.

Condamine River downstream of Warwick to Oakey Creek:
Minor flooding is occurring at Tummaville. Minor flooding is continuing along Hodgson
Creek at Felton and along the Condamine River at Millbrook.

The Condamine River at Tummaville is currently at 5.66 m and rising, with minor flooding.
The Condamine River at Tummaville is likely to remain above the minor flood level (5.00 m)
Saturday into Sunday. The river may peak near 5.70 m Saturday afternoon, with minor
flooding.

Safety Advice:
* Don't drive, walk, swim or play in floodwater because it is dangerous.
* Stay away from flooded drains, rivers, streams and waterways.
* Obey road closure signs. Plan ahead so you don't drive on flooded roads.
* Check the ABC and local media for updates. The situation can change quickly, so stay
informed.
* For local emergency management warnings and advice visit
www.disaster.qld.gov.au/warnings.

For emergency assistance call SES on telephone number 132 500. In life-threatening
emergencies, call 000 (triple zero) immediately.

Next issue:
The next warning will be issued by 03:00 PM AEST on Sunday 15 November 2026.
