About Essential Energy

Essential Energy is the electricity distribution network for parts of NSW, QLD — it owns and maintains the poles, wires and substations that carry power to roughly 3,754 suburbs we track. It is not an energy retailer: which distributor serves you is decided by your address, not by the company that bills you.

We collect Essential Energy's publicly published outage information continuously and show it alongside the other connected networks, so you do not have to know whose network you are on before you can find out whether your power is out. Essential Energy remains the authority on its own network.
