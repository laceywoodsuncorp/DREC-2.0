🛰️ Active Fire Map — Australia

NASA FIRMS active fire detections and NASA SEDAC population density, plotted on an OpenStreetMap street basemap so town and road names stay visible. Colour scales from red (detected in the last few hours) to green (up to 5 days old), and older detections fade so the most recent fires stand out. Satellite passes are processed with a 3–6 hour lag, so the newest detections available are a few hours old.
🔑 Live fire detections come from NASA FIRMS. Your MAP_KEY is pre-loaded, so hotspots load automatically — press Load fires to refresh. Need your own key? Get one free at firms.modaps.eosdis.nasa.gov/api/map_key. Detections are fetched as points from the FIRMS API and coloured by time since detection.

About this dashboard: News headlines come live from each outlet’s own RSS feed — national and wire services (ABC, SBS, Nine, 7NEWS, The Australian, Guardian Australia, AAP, The New Daily, The Conversation), the capital-city mastheads (Sydney Morning Herald, The Age, Brisbane Times, WAtoday, The West Australian), regional and rural papers (Canberra Times, Newcastle Herald, Illawarra Mercury, The Examiner, The Advocate, The Border Mail, The Courier, Bendigo Advertiser, Northern Daily Leader, Daily Advertiser, Central Western Daily, The Land), insuranceNEWS.com.au for trade coverage, and Al Jazeera for world news — fetched server-side, merged and de-duplicated. The feed shows the last 24 hours, or the most recent available if it has been quieter than that; each headline carries its own age. Some mastheads (notably The Australian) are subscriber-only, so following those links may hit a paywall. The map uses NASA FIRMS for active-fire detections (free MAP_KEY required), plotted on an OpenStreetMap street basemap so town and road names stay visible; colour and opacity scale by time since detection. State emergency incident data is live for all eight states and territories, each from its own official agency feed (NSW RFS, QFES, VicEmergency, Emergency WA, SA CFS, Tasmania Fire Service, NT PFES, ACT ESA), proxied server-side with no API key; if a feed is temporarily unreachable that state says so and links through to its official current-incidents page rather than showing older data. The town explorer is powered by CensusAtlas (censusatlas.au), an independent visualisation of the 2011/2016/2021 ABS Census used under CC BY 4.0 — embedded and deep-linked here; the quick-snapshot figures are a small local sample (extendable via CSV import) for at-a-glance use. Styling uses the Suncorp brand colour palette.
